Noir Voyage: Connect With Your Roots, Travel Your Way

Noir Voyage is a community driven travel booking platform designed specifically for university students particularly Black Afro-British students and those from diverse cultural backgrounds who want to explore the world while deepening their connection to their heritage.

Unlike traditional travel agencies that focus on generic tourist traps, Noir Voyage curates culturally significant destinations across Africa, the Caribbean, and global diaspora hubs. We understand the financial constraints of student life, which is why we merge exclusive student discounts with social, peer to peer group travel, making meaningful exploration both accessible and highly collaborative.

Core Value Proposition

For the culturally curious student, Noir Voyage is the bridge between affordable travel and authentic heritage exploration. We eliminate the isolation of solo travel and the high costs of international trips by empowering students to find discounted flights, build group itineraries, and connect with a verified community of like minded peers before they even pack their bags.

Target Audience
Our primary users are UK-based university students looking for more than just a standard holiday. They are budget-conscious, socially active, and highly motivated to learn about their roots, seeking out destinations like London, Accra, Lagos, New York, and Cape Town.
